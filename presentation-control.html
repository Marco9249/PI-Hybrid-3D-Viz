BLOCK 4 — MPC MANAGER & CONTROLLER

Hierarchical MPC System

The brain of the system. Uses AI forecasts to manage water proactively.

1. MPC MANAGER (STRATEGY)
🔴 IDLE
Condition: P_available < 2400W
Action: Pump OFF, monitors GHI every 60s.
Exit: P ≥ 2400W sustained for 300s.
🟡 STARTING
Duration: 5 min stable power.
Why: Prevents motor short-cycling at cloud edges.
🟢 RUNNING
Action: Sends Flow_Ref to MPC Controller.
If Future_GHI drops AND Tank < Safe_Level → Flow_Ref = Max (store water early).
🔵 BRAKING
Formula: Flow = Flow_max × √(1 − Level/Max_Level)
Why: Gradual slowdown prevents hydraulic hammer.
2. MPC CONTROLLER (EXECUTION)
Role: Computes the optimal Speed_Ref over a 300-step future horizon based on the Manager's Flow_Ref.
