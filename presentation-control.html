BLOCK 6 — VARIABLE FREQUENCY DRIVE

VFD: Electrical-Physical Bridge

Converts the FOPID control signal into precisely regulated motor frequency — the critical interface between the digital control world and physical pump mechanics.

⚙️ VFD Input Processing

▼
Input: \( P_{control} \) (0–100%).
Soft Start: Overcomes stiction, prevents current spikes.
Rate Limiter: Prevents mechanical stress.

🔴 Dead Zone — <690 RPM

▼
Cut to zero. Why: Pumps rely on rotation for cooling. Running too slow melts motor windings.

🟡 Saturation — >1550 RPM

▼
Speed capped. Why: Prevents Cavitation — vapor bubbles that violently erode impeller blades.

BLOCK 7 — PUMP + GRAVITY TANK

Closing the Loop

Pump converts RPM to Flow. Tank integrates Flow to Level. Level feeds back to MPC Manager, completing the loop.

💧 Pump Model

▼
Converts RPM to flow rate (L/s):
$$ Q_{in} = k_{pump} \cdot (RPM - RPM_{min}) $$
Fast evaluation model for MPC.

🏗️ Tank — Integral Block

▼
$$ h(t) = \int \frac{Q_{in} - Q_{demand}}{A} dt $$
Max: 9800 L. Triggers BRAKING state near max.

♻️ Gravity Battery

▼
Gravitational Energy Storage: Converts solar to hydraulic potential. Cheaper & longer-lasting than lithium batteries.
